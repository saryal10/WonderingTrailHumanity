Our Philosophy

Tourism Shouldn't Just Take From Places. It Should Give Back.

Wandering Trails Humanities bridges international volunteers with remote communities in Nepal. We believe travel is most transformative when built upon genuine connection and reciprocal growth.

Rather than transactional volunteering, every initiative blends meaningful service projects—such as youth workshops, health post support, and eco-tourism—with local homestays and expert-guided Himalayan trekking.

Featured Expedition

14-Day Gorkha Trek & Serve Itinerary

A detailed breakdown of our 2-week signature journey combining orientation, direct village service, cultural exchange, and Himalayan trekking.

Days 1 - 2 Kathmandu Valley

Arrival, Cultural Orientation & Nepali Language Prep

Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM). Meet the Wandering Trails team and transfer to hotel. Day 2 includes comprehensive safety briefings, introductory Nepali language lessons, and a guided visit to iconic cultural landmarks including Boudhanath Stupa and Pashupatinath.

Days 3 - 4 Kathmandu to Gorkha District

Overland Journey to Gorkha & Host Family Welcome

Scenic overland drive from Kathmandu into the lush mid-hill ridges of Gorkha. Tour historic Gorkha Durbar (palace) overlooking the Manaslu range. Meet your local host family, settle into the village homestay, and participate in a traditional welcome dinner.

Days 5 - 9 Remote Gorkha Village

Hands-On Community Service & School Workshops

Engage in core community service projects designed directly with local village leaders:

  • Facilitating art, storytelling, and conversational English workshops at the local school.
  • Participating in school renovation, painting, or clean drinking water setup.
  • Organizing health awareness sessions or youth sports exchanges.
  • Evening cultural exchanges and cooking traditional Nepalese meals with host families.
Days 10 - 12 Gorkha Foothills & Ridge Trek

Guided Himalayan Ridge Trekking & Panoramic Views

Embark on a 3-day guided eco-trek through pristine rural trails and rhododendron forests with views of Mount Manaslu and Ganesh Himal. Stay in local tea houses, experience remote mountain hospitality, and learn about flora and mountain ecology.

Days 13 - 14 Return to Kathmandu & Departure

Reflection, Farewell Ceremony & Airport Transfer

Return drive to Kathmandu. Group reflection session and farewell dinner featuring traditional Nepali dance and music. Final shopping in Thamel and airport transfer for international flights.
