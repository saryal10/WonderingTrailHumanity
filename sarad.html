Sarad Aryal

Tourism Entrepreneur / Engineer

Sarad Aryal serves as the International Outreach Lead (USA) for Wandering Trails Humanity. Bridging technical precision with a passion for experiential travel, he brings a unique perspective as both an engineer and a seasoned tourism entrepreneur.

Based internationally, Sarad plays a pivotal role in expanding global partnerships, connecting international volunteers and supporters with impactful service-learning initiatives, and championing ethical community development across Nepal.

His structural background in engineering combined with industry expertise allows him to help guide sustainable expedition frameworks, ensuring that every journey supports long-term local empowerment and community resilience.
