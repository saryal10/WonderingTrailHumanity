Your Trip Has a Purpose

Leave the crowds of Sauraha behind and journey deep into the quieter landscapes of Madi Valley. Sleep beneath the stars on a traditional elevated Machan, share meaningful moments with children at a rural community school, watch the sun disappear where the Rapti and Narayani rivers meet, and explore the wetlands of Bishazari Tal.

Community Reinvestment: Wandering Trail Humanity reinvests the profit from this experience directly into future community camps, educational workshops, health, dental, and eye initiatives for underserved rural schools in Nepal.

🌿 Your 4-Day Journey

Day 1 Kathmandu → Madi Valley

Into the Hidden Side of Chitwan

Your journey begins with a private pickup from your Kathmandu hotel. Leave the busy capital behind and travel south toward Chitwan, gradually moving into quieter countryside and rural communities. Continue into Madi Valley, one of the less-visited areas of Chitwan. Settle into your tent set on a traditional elevated wooden Machan platform. Dinner is served at camp.

Overnight: Machan Tent Camp, Madi
Day 2 Community School Immersion

Community, Connection & Purpose

Travel to a local community school serving an underprivileged community. Host customized workshops tailored around your skills, profession, or passion:

🎨 Art & Creativity
📚 Education & English
💻 Digital & Computers
🩺 Health & Hygiene
⚽ Sports & Games
🌱 Environment
📷 Photography
💡 Career Skills
Overnight: Machan Tent Camp, Madi
Day 3 Madi → Golghat → Patihani

Community Impact, Golghat Sunset & Patihani

Return to the school for your second day of workshop activities and complete your community project. Continue toward Golghat to watch the sun sink over the river landscape where the Rapti and Narayani rivers meet. Afterward, transfer to Patihani and check into your hotel.

Overnight: Hotel in Patihani
Day 4 Bishazari Tal → Kathmandu

Bishazari Tal Wildlife Exploration & Return

Head toward Bishazari Tal (Twenty Thousand Lakes). Explore the protected wetlands by private vehicle with your guide searching for rhinos, deer, crocodiles, monkeys, and birds of prey. Enjoy lunch before returning to Kathmandu for hotel drop-off.

Days 1 - 2 Kathmandu Valley

Arrival, Cultural Orientation & Nepali Language Prep

Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM). Meet the Wandering Trails team and transfer to hotel. Day 2 includes comprehensive safety briefings, introductory Nepali language lessons, and a guided visit to iconic cultural landmarks including Boudhanath Stupa and Pashupatinath.

Days 3 - 4 Kathmandu to Expedition Region

Overland Journey & Host Family Welcome

Scenic overland drive from Kathmandu into the lush mid-hill ridges of rural Nepal. Tour historic local heritage sites overlooking Himalayan mountain ranges. Meet your local host family, settle into the village homestay, and participate in a traditional welcome dinner.

Days 5 - 9 Remote Village Community

Hands-On Community Service & School Workshops

Engage in core community service projects designed directly with local village leaders:

  • Facilitating art, storytelling, and conversational English workshops at the local school.
  • Participating in school renovation, painting, or clean drinking water setup.
  • Organizing health awareness sessions, specialty clinics, or youth sports exchanges.
  • Evening cultural exchanges and cooking traditional Nepalese meals with host families.
Days 10 - 12 Foothills & Himalayan Ridge Trek

Guided Himalayan Ridge Trekking & Panoramic Views

Embark on a guided eco-trek through pristine rural trails and rhododendron forests with views of snow-capped Himalayan peaks. Stay in local tea houses, experience mountain hospitality, and learn about flora and mountain ecology.

Days 13 - 15 Return to Kathmandu & Departure

Reflection, Farewell Ceremony & Airport Transfer

Return drive to Kathmandu. Group reflection session and farewell dinner featuring traditional Nepali dance and music. Final shopping in Thamel and airport transfer for international flights.
